Privacy Policy

How WhatToPack handles your data.

Information we collect

Trip details, packing items and destination information stay on your device. If you create an optional profile, your name and phone number are also stored locally in the app's storage only — that information is not sent to us or to any server.

Shared checklists

The shared checklist feature is the exception: when you invite someone to a checklist, the app uses Google Firebase to sync that specific checklist's items between the people sharing it. It is deleted from Firestore as soon as the share is ended.

Third-party services

  • Google Places API — destination search and autocomplete
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Firebase — shared checklist sync (only when you use that feature)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. WhatToPack does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Your choices

  • Disable reminder notifications at any time in the app or your device settings.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.
  • Leave or end a shared checklist from within the app.
  • Delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Data retention

Local data lives on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Shared checklist data in Firestore is temporary and is deleted once the share ends.
