Weather-aware planning

Pack for the trip you're actually taking.

WhatToPack pulls in real forecasts — live weather for near-term trips, and seasonal climate trends when your trip is further out — via Open-Meteo, so suggestions reflect actual conditions instead of a generic seasonal guess.

  • Live forecasts for trips coming up soon
  • Seasonal climate trends for trips further out
  • A plain-English line explaining what the forecast means for packing

Weather data is a helpful guide, not a guarantee — always double-check conditions closer to your travel date.

Shared checklists

Pack together. Stay in sync.

Share a packing list with someone using an invite code and pack from the same checklist. When either of you checks off an item, it updates on the other's phone — useful for families, couples and any trip you're not taking alone.

  • Invite someone with a simple code
  • Checked items sync to everyone on the list
  • Works offline and reconciles once you're back online
Home-screen widget

Your next trip, one glance away.

The packing widget keeps your next trip visible on your home screen — how many days until you leave, and how much of your list is still unpacked.

  • Next trip and countdown
  • Packing progress at a glance
