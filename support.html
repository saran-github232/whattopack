Support

Troubleshooting tips and how to get help.

Notifications aren't showing up

Check that notifications are enabled for WhatToPack in your device settings. Battery optimization can also stop reminders from firing — allow the app to run unrestricted if reminders aren't arriving.

Destination search isn't finding my place

Destination search needs an internet connection. Try entering at least a few characters of the place name.

Weather-based suggestions seem off

Suggestions are based on the forecast available when you create the trip. Treat them as a helpful starting point, and always double-check conditions closer to your travel date.

My data or trip is missing

Trip and profile data is stored locally on your device. If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, this local data won't carry over and can't be recovered.

About the ads

WhatToPack is ad-supported. If you see a problematic or inappropriate ad, let us know using the contact below.
